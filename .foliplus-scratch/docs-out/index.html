foliplus#

foliplus: the missing piece between “see the map” and “use the map”. GeoPandas handles data, Folium renders it, foliplus brings it to life.

Scope#

Layer data passed to foliplus is read-only: no editing, upload, or deletion.

Data processing happens upstream. foliplus handles the map, not the data.

Features#

📷 ExportControl

Capture a specific area of the map and export it as an image.

api/foliplus.ExportControl.html
🖥️ FullscreenControl

Fullscreen toggle with auto-hide for other controls.

api/foliplus.FullscreenControl.html
🔥 HeatmapControl

H3 hexbin heatmap with zoom-adaptive resolution and labeled hexagons.

api/foliplus.HeatmapControl.html
🗂️ LayerControl

Layer panel to organize, inspect, style, and filter map layers.

api/foliplus.LayerControl.html
🎯 LocateControl

Fly to the user’s current position.

api/foliplus.LocateControl.html
📏 MeasureControl

Measure distances, areas, and circles; place geocoded markers; then edit by dragging nodes.

api/foliplus.MeasureControl.html
📐 ScaleControl

Scale bar with metric units and optional zoom level display.

api/foliplus.ScaleControl.html
🔍 SearchControl

Coordinate and address search via pluggable geocode providers.

api/foliplus.SearchControl.html
