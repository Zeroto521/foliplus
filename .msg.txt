feat(LayerControl): a container row expands its child layers in place

A layer group's chevron now reveals its child layers as operable rows —
each with its own visibility checkbox, label and type glyph — capped at
fifty children per group. The expanded state persists with the map record
(version 6 to 7: a new expandedContainers map; older records read as
empty). Child visibility writes go straight to the map, so the group's
own count and the role tri-state keep their single sources.

Child rows render checkbox, label and type icon only in this first step;
per-child menus (focus, rename, delete) follow in a later change.
